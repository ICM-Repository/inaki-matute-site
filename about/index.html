About Inaki Matute.

Network architect, former CTO, and the person behind Aisla. Based in North Vancouver, BC.

Thirty years, in order

  1. 2023 to now: Managing Director, Webrij Services, Vancouver. AWS, web, mobile, AI microservices, CI/CD and security. Read more.
  2. 2016 to 2023: Chief Technology Consultant, DataBridge Asia, Makati. National motor vehicle registration system. Read more.
  3. 2009 to 2019: Chief Technology Officer, e-Pass Philippines, Manila. First electronic toll collection platform on SLEX. Read more.
  4. 2007 to 2009: VP Technology and Special Projects, PhilWeb, Manila. Technology provider for PAGCOR, 115 locations. Read more.
  5. 2006 to 2007: Senior Project Manager, PowerConnex, Australia. Sixteen-person team, contact-centre infrastructure.
  6. 1998 to 2005: Administrator and Internet Security Manager, Jupitermedia, USA. Hardware Central forums.

  • Python, React, Node.js, PyTorch, OpenAI APIs, Docker and CI/CD.
  • Team direction, Agile and DevOps, IT governance and vendor management.
