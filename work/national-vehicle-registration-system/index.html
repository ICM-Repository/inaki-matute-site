The story

From 2016 to 2023 I was Chief Technology Consultant at DataBridge Asia in Makati, working on the architecture and systems integration of the Philippines' national motor vehicle registration system.

It is a national-scale public-sector system, and the work covered both its architecture and the integration of the systems around it.

This is the experience behind my architecture work today and behind Aisla.
