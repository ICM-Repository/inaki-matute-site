e-Pass: electronic toll collection that never closes.

The first electronic toll platform on SLEX, built with Kapsch and Hopetech.

At a glance

  • Chief Technology Officer, e-Pass Philippines, 2009 to 2019
  • 80M+ toll passes a year
  • $280M+ in toll revenue
  • Running 24 hours a day, every day

The story

A toll road is a system that earns every second it is up, and loses every second it is down. As CTO of e-Pass Philippines from 2009 to 2019 I was responsible for the technology of the first electronic toll collection platform on SLEX, built together with Kapsch and Hopetech.

The platform handled more than eighty million passes a year and moved more than $280 million in toll revenue through it, with the uptime expectation of a utility: around the clock.

This is the experience behind my architecture work today and behind Aisla.
