An AI front desk for clinics that answers at 9pm.

Patients ask questions when the clinic is closed. Aisla builds the front door that answers them, books them in and follows up, without replacing the tools you already use.

What the front desk does

  • Answers inquiries day and night, in your clinic's words.
  • Handles booking and reminders, so fewer slots go empty.
  • Follows up automatically with people who asked but did not book.
  • Works inside your tools: Gmail, Google Workspace, WhatsApp, Messenger and more.

Built for the way clinics actually run

Aisla starts with one small, real win: a call to find where the daily leak is, then a first live result in 5 to 7 days. The full front door follows within 2 to 3 weeks.

Every build includes hands-on training on your real system, an owner walkthrough, a written playbook and a check-in at week two, so the staff who use it every day are the ones who trust it.

Privacy: clinical data is never stored or processed. You keep your data, your accounts and your domain.

The front desk is managed for you after launch, and custom builds or an ongoing operations partnership are available if you want to go further.
